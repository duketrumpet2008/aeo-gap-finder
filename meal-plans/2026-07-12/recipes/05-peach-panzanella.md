# Peach Panzanella (Burrata, Basil and Peach Panzanella)

**Source:** [How Sweet Eats (Jessica Merchant)](https://www.howsweeteats.com/2021/08/peach-panzanella/)
> ⚠️ Transcribed from memory — the source site was unreachable from this environment. Verify quantities against the link above before shopping/cooking.

**Serves:** 4 (as a side or light main — published serving count could not be verified)
**Time:** ~15 min prep / 10–15 min bread toasting / ~30 min total
**Meal slot:** Monday dinner (roast chicken leftovers = Tuesday dinner)

A summer panzanella: toasty bread cubes soaked with a blender basil vinaigrette, juicy peach slices, tomatoes, and burrata torn over the top. The vinaigrette is bright and slightly briny from the lemon and vinegar.

## Ingredients

*Salad:*
- 3 to 4 cups cubed crusty bread (sourdough or ciabatta), in ~1-inch cubes *(quantity approximate — verify)*
- 2 ripe peaches, thinly sliced
- 1 cup cherry or grape tomatoes, halved *(quantity approximate — verify)*
- 8 ounces burrata cheese
- Fresh basil leaves, for topping
- Flaked sea salt and freshly cracked black pepper, for finishing

*Basil vinaigrette (blender):*
- 1 cup loosely packed fresh basil leaves *(quantity approximate — verify)*
- 1 small garlic clove
- 2 tablespoons red wine vinegar *(approximate — verify)*
- 1 tablespoon freshly squeezed lemon juice *(the published dressing is "slightly briny from the lemon and vinegar")*
- 1 tablespoon honey *(approximate — verify)*
- ¼ teaspoon salt and ¼ teaspoon pepper
- ⅓ to ½ cup extra-virgin olive oil *(approximate — verify)*

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Spread the bread cubes on a baking sheet and bake for 10 to 15 minutes, until they are slightly toasty and golden on the edges but still a bit chewy inside. Let cool slightly.
2. Meanwhile, make the basil vinaigrette: add all the vinaigrette ingredients to a blender or food processor and blend until smooth and emulsified, scraping down as needed.
3. Place the toasted bread cubes in a large bowl or on a large platter. Drizzle about 2 to 3 tablespoons of the basil vinaigrette over the cubes and toss well so the dressing soaks into the bread.
4. Add the peach slices and the halved tomatoes with a pinch of salt and pepper, and toss gently to combine.
5. Set the burrata on top (either pull it apart and toss it fully into the panzanella, or leave it whole on top and pull it apart as you serve). Drizzle with another spoonful or two of the vinaigrette.
6. Top with fresh basil leaves, finish with flaked salt and cracked pepper, and serve immediately, passing any remaining vinaigrette alongside.

## Prep-ahead & notes
- **Make ahead:** the basil vinaigrette can be blended up to 3–4 days ahead and refrigerated in a sealed jar (bring to room temperature and shake before using). Bread cubes can be toasted several hours ahead and kept uncovered at room temperature.
- **Assemble at the last minute.** Once dressed, the bread softens and the peaches weep — this salad does not keep well; leftovers get soggy within a few hours. Slice the peaches just before assembling.
- Peak-season, slightly firm-ripe peaches hold their shape best. No need to peel.
