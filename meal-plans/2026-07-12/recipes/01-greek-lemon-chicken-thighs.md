# Greek Chicken Thighs
**Source:** [Lite Cravings (Geri)](https://litecravings.com/greek-chicken-thighs/)
> ✅ Verified against the published recipe (print-to-PDF provided by Alex, 7/11/26).
**Serves:** 6 (174 kcal each)
**Time:** 1 hr prep (incl. minimum marinade) / 15 min cook / 1 hr 15 min total — marinade holds up to 24 hr
**Meal slot:** Sunday dinner — Greek Feast (leftovers = Monday lunch)

## Ingredients

**For the marinade**
- 6 medium skinless chicken thighs (about 2 pounds for boneless or 3 pounds for bone-in)
- 5 garlic cloves, crushed
- ¼ cup extra virgin olive oil
- Zest and juice of 1 large lemon
- 2 tbsp water or chicken stock
- 1½ tsp dried oregano
- 1½ tsp kosher salt
- ½ tsp cracked black pepper
- ½ tsp cayenne pepper (optional, if you want some spice)

**For serving**
- ¼ cup chopped parsley
- 3 oz feta, crumbled
- Extra lemon slices

## Instructions
1. Mix all marinade ingredients together. Add chicken and coat evenly.
2. Refrigerate chicken in marinade at least an hour or up to 24 hours.
3. Heat a large oven-proof skillet to medium-high. Preheat oven to 475°F.
4. Remove chicken from marinade and lightly pat dry with a towel. Grease skillet with nonstick spray, then add chicken, leaving room between each thigh so the chicken can brown properly. Discard marinade.
5. Sear for about 4–5 minutes, or until that side of the chicken is golden brown. Flip chicken and place lemon slices throughout the skillet so they caramelize while cooking. Place skillet in oven.
6. If using bone-in thighs, bake for 10–12 minutes, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
7. If using boneless thighs, bake for 7–10 minutes, or until internal temperature reaches 165°F.
8. Remove chicken from oven and let rest for at least 5 minutes before serving. Sprinkle evenly with parsley and feta.

## Prep-ahead & notes
- **Marinating window:** at least 1 hour, up to 24 hours — for this week, get the chicken in right after the Sunday-morning shop (~11:30 am) for a 5:30 pm cook.
- WW-friendly: 5 SmartPoints per thigh with the feta (calculated with boneless skinless thighs).
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days refrigerated; tuck into pita with hummus for Monday lunch.
