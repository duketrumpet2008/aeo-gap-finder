# Greek Chicken Thighs
**Source:** [Lite Cravings](https://litecravings.com/greek-chicken-thighs/)
> ⚠️ Transcribed from memory — the source site was unreachable from this environment. Verify quantities against the link above before shopping/cooking.
**Serves:** 4 (best recall — verify at source)
**Time:** ~10 min prep / ~20 min cook / plus 1–24 hours marinating (overnight is best)
**Meal slot:** Sunday dinner — Greek Feast (leftovers = Monday lunch)

*Note: the cooking method below (times, temperatures, and technique) follows the Lite Cravings recipe closely; the ingredient **quantities** are a faithful-style reconstruction of a standard Greek lemon-garlic-oregano marinade — the blog's exact amounts could not be retrieved. The marinade components (garlic, lemon juice and zest, oregano, salt, pepper, a little olive oil) and the feta/parsley finish match the source.*

## Ingredients
- 1½–2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken thighs (bone-in thighs also work — see step 6 for timing)
- 3–4 cloves garlic, minced
- Juice and zest of 1 large lemon (about ¼ cup juice), plus 1 additional lemon, sliced into rounds
- 1 tablespoon olive oil
- 1½ teaspoons dried oregano
- 1 teaspoon kosher salt
- ½ teaspoon black pepper
- Nonstick cooking spray, for the skillet
- ¼ cup crumbled feta cheese, for serving
- 2 tablespoons chopped fresh parsley, for serving

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl or zip-top bag, mix all of the marinade ingredients together: minced garlic, lemon juice and zest, olive oil, oregano, salt, and pepper.
2. Add the chicken thighs and toss to coat evenly in the marinade. Cover (or seal the bag) and refrigerate for at least 1 hour and up to 24 hours — overnight gives the best flavor, but even an hour or two works well.
3. When ready to cook, heat a large oven-proof skillet over medium-high heat and preheat the oven to 475°F.
4. Remove the chicken from the marinade and lightly pat it dry with a paper towel (this helps it sear rather than steam). Discard the remaining marinade.
5. Grease the hot skillet with nonstick spray and add the chicken, leaving a little room between each thigh. Sear for about 4–5 minutes, without moving, until golden brown on the first side.
6. Flip the chicken and tuck the lemon slices throughout the skillet. Transfer the skillet to the oven. If using bone-in thighs, bake for 10–12 minutes; if using boneless thighs, bake for 7–10 minutes — until the internal temperature reaches 165°F.
7. Remove from the oven and sprinkle evenly with the chopped parsley and crumbled feta. Serve with the roasted lemon slices.

## Prep-ahead & notes
- **Marinating window:** at least 1 hour, up to 24 hours; overnight is ideal. You can mix the marinade and start the chicken the night before the feast.
- The feta and parsley go on after cooking — prep them ahead and keep refrigerated.
- Leftovers keep 3–4 days refrigerated in an airtight container; slice cold over a salad or tuck into pita with hummus for Monday lunch.
- Pat the chicken dry before searing — a good sear at medium-high plus the hot 475°F oven is what keeps the thighs juicy with a golden crust.
